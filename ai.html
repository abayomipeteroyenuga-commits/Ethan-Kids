ETHAN KIDS · NEW LEARNING LAB

AI & Smart Technology

Patterns, predictions, errors and responsible use. Learn one idea, practise it, solve a mission and explain your thinking.

Explore the modules ↓
🤖

How to learn here

Discover → Try → Solve → Create → Explain. Each module includes a clear idea, a practical example and a mission. Take your time; mistakes help you improve.

Explore 5 learning modules

MODULE 01

What is AI?

AI systems can find patterns in examples and produce predictions or content.

Try it: A photo tool groups pictures of cats and dogs.

Remember: A prediction is not the same as a fact.

MODULE 02

Training examples

Examples influence what a model learns.

Try it: A model trained only on red apples might miss green apples.

Remember: Varied examples help test whether a model generalises.

MODULE 03

AI mistakes

AI may give an answer that sounds confident but is wrong.

Try it: Check an AI claim against a trusted book or teacher.

Remember: Verify important information independently.

MODULE 04

Privacy

Avoid entering names, addresses, passwords or personal photos into unfamiliar tools.

Try it: Replace a real name with a fictional character in a practice prompt.

Remember: Protect personal information.

MODULE 05

Human decisions

People should review AI suggestions and decide what to use.

Try it: Compare two suggestions and explain your choice.

Remember: AI can assist, but people remain responsible.
