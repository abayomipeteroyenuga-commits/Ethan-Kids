ETHAN KIDS · NEW LEARNING LAB

Digital Art & Animation

Frames, layers, motion and accessible design. Learn one idea, practise it, solve a mission and explain your thinking.

Explore the modules ↓
🎨

How to learn here

Discover → Try → Solve → Create → Explain. Each module includes a clear idea, a practical example and a mission. Take your time; mistakes help you improve.

Explore 5 learning modules

MODULE 01

Pixels and shapes

Images can be made from small coloured squares or shapes.

Try it: Draw a face using circles and rectangles.

Remember: Pixels and shapes are building blocks of digital art.

MODULE 02

Layers

Place objects in front of or behind each other.

Try it: Move the sun behind a mountain.

Remember: Layer order changes what we see.

MODULE 03

Frames

Animation shows a sequence of pictures with small changes.

Try it: Move a ball a little to the right in each frame.

Remember: Small changes across frames create motion.

MODULE 04

Timing

The speed of frames changes how movement feels.

Try it: Compare a ball moving 2 steps versus 8 steps each frame.

Remember: Timing affects the story.

MODULE 05

Readable design

Choose readable text, clear icons and colours with enough contrast.

Try it: Compare dark text on pale yellow with yellow text on white.

Remember: Good design helps everyone use your creation.
