My First App Studio

Design a useful mini-app with screens, buttons, feedback and testing.

LESSON 1 OF 5

App purpose

An app helps a person do something. Decide who it helps and what one task it should make easier.

Try it: Write one sentence describing your app’s purpose.

Stretch challenge: Design a study helper for a classmate.

LESSON 2 OF 5

Screens and navigation

A screen shows information and actions. Clear labels help people know where a button will take them.

Try it: Give each screen a short title and one clear action.

Stretch challenge: Create a home screen and a results screen.

LESSON 3 OF 5

Input and output

Input is what a user provides; output is what the app shows back. Good feedback explains what happened.

Try it: Enter a short, non-personal message and preview the response.

Stretch challenge: Create a greeting app that reacts to an input.

LESSON 4 OF 5

Conditions

A condition helps an app respond differently. IF a field is empty, ask for input; ELSE show a result.

Try it: Test the app with an empty input and a filled input.

Stretch challenge: Add helpful empty-input feedback.

LESSON 5 OF 5

Testing and inclusion

Try the app with a keyboard, readable text and different screen sizes. Fix one confusing part.

Try it: Ask: can someone understand each button without guessing?

Stretch challenge: Improve your app based on three test cases.
