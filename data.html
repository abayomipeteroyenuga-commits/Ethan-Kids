ETHAN KIDS · NEW LEARNING LAB

Data Detective

Questions, counting, charts and evidence. Learn one idea, practise it, solve a mission and explain your thinking.

Explore the modules ↓
📊

How to learn here

Discover → Try → Solve → Create → Explain. Each module includes a clear idea, a practical example and a mission. Take your time; mistakes help you improve.

Explore 5 learning modules

MODULE 01

Ask a question

Choose a question that can be answered by counting.

Try it: Which fictional fruit is most popular in a pretend class?

Remember: A clear question guides data collection.

MODULE 02

Tally and count

Group observations and count carefully.

Try it: Apple, pear, apple, banana → apple 2.

Remember: Counts help organise information.

MODULE 03

Charts

Choose a chart that makes comparisons clear.

Try it: Make a bar chart for apple 4, pear 2, banana 3.

Remember: Label categories and use a sensible scale.

MODULE 04

Interpret

Explain what a chart shows without guessing extra facts.

Try it: A bar of 4 is higher than a bar of 2.

Remember: A chart shows only the data collected.

MODULE 05

Fair conclusions

Check whether the sample answers the question.

Try it: Five votes cannot tell you what every child likes.

Remember: Small samples have limits.
