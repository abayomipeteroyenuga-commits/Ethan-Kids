Turn an idea into an invention

1 · Notice a real need

Look around your home or classroom. Ask: what is difficult, confusing or slow?

Your mission: Choose a small problem, such as organising pencils or remembering homework.

2 · Ask useful questions

Who experiences the problem? When does it happen? What would a helpful solution do?

Your mission: Write three questions that could help you understand the problem.

3 · Sketch three ideas

There is rarely only one possible solution. Draw alternatives before choosing.

Your mission: Draw three different designs. Label what each part does.

4 · Make a simple prototype

A prototype is an early model. Use paper, drawings or a digital mock-up to show how it might work.

Your mission: Make a paper model or design a screen in Web Craft.

5 · Test with a checklist

Check whether the design works for its purpose. Use clear criteria rather than “I like it.”

Your mission: Choose three tests: easy to understand, useful, and safe to use.

6 · Improve and explain

Use what you learned from testing to change one feature at a time.

Your mission: Write: “I changed ___ because ___; now ___.”
