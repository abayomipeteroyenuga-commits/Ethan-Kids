Music & Sound Coding

Explore rhythm, sequences and events through a playable musical keyboard.

Start learning ↓

LESSON 1 OF 5

Notes

Notes are sounds with different pitches. A melody is an ordered sequence of notes.

Try it: Play three notes and describe whether the pitch goes up or down.

Stretch challenge: Make a three-note melody.

LESSON 2 OF 5

Rhythm

Rhythm is the pattern of sound and silence over time. Even a short pause changes a tune.

Try it: Try the same notes with different pauses.

Stretch challenge: Compose a four-beat rhythm.

LESSON 3 OF 5

Loops in music

Repeated musical phrases are like loops in code. A repeated pattern can make a song easier to remember.

Try it: Play a phrase twice.

Stretch challenge: Create an eight-note tune using a repeated phrase.

LESSON 4 OF 5

Events

An event starts an action. Pressing a piano key triggers a sound; releasing it ends that action.

Try it: Press different keys and observe the response.

Stretch challenge: Create a keyboard performance.

LESSON 5 OF 5

Composition and testing

Compose, listen, revise. Change one note or pause and decide how the new version sounds.

Try it: Make two versions of your melody.

Stretch challenge: Save a final eight-note tune to My Creations.
