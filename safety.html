ETHAN KIDS · NEW LEARNING LAB

Cyber Safety

Privacy, passwords, kindness and asking for help. Learn one idea, practise it, solve a mission and explain your thinking.

Explore the modules ↓
🛡️

How to learn here

Discover → Try → Solve → Create → Explain. Each module includes a clear idea, a practical example and a mission. Take your time; mistakes help you improve.

Explore 5 learning modules

MODULE 01

Private information

Know which details should stay private.

Try it: Choose a fictional username instead of your full name.

Remember: Keep passwords, addresses and contact details private.

MODULE 02

Strong passwords

Use long unique passwords and ask a trusted adult about a password manager.

Try it: Identify which example is longer and less predictable.

Remember: Never share your password with friends.

MODULE 03

Suspicious messages

Pause before opening unexpected links or attachments.

Try it: A message says you won a prize and must click now: ask a trusted adult.

Remember: Urgency and unexpected requests are warning signs.

MODULE 04

Respect online

Be kind, get permission before sharing someone else’s work and report bullying.

Try it: Rewrite an unkind comment into constructive feedback.

Remember: Respect applies online and offline.

MODULE 05

Ask for help

Know when to stop and tell a trusted adult or teacher.

Try it: A stranger requests personal details: do not reply; tell a trusted adult.

Remember: You do not have to solve online problems alone.
