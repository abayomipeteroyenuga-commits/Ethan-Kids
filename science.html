Science & Discovery Lab

Ask questions, observe carefully, measure, record data and explain what you find.

Explore how the world works

1 · Observe

Scientists begin with questions. Look closely at an everyday object and describe its color, shape, size and material. Record what you see, not what you guess.

Try: Compare two leaves or two safe household objects without tasting or mixing anything.

2 · Predict and test

A prediction is what you think will happen. A fair test changes one thing at a time while keeping other conditions the same.

Try: Which paper airplane design travels farther? Ask an adult before testing in an open, safe area.

3 · Measure and record

Measurements use units. Length can be measured in centimetres and time in seconds. Record each result before drawing conclusions.

Try: Measure three books and compare their lengths.

4 · Spot patterns

Data is information you collect. A table makes it easier to notice patterns, differences and unusual results.

Try: Count different colors of objects on a desk and make a tally chart.
