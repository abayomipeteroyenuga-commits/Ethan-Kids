Practise writing, creative decisions and interactive storytelling with your own original characters.

Make your story come alive

1 · Build a character

Give your character a goal, a strength and a problem to solve. A good story shows how choices change what happens.

Try: Describe a curious robot in three sentences.

2 · Plan a beginning, middle and end

Introduce a place and character, present a challenge, then show how the character responds. Use short paragraphs and clear words.

Try: Write a five-sentence story before making it interactive.

3 · Branching choices

Interactive stories let readers choose what happens next. Each choice should lead to a different consequence.

Try: Make a map with two choices and two endings.

4 · Revise and share safely

Read your story aloud, check spelling and ask whether each choice makes sense. Do not include private details about yourself or anyone else.

Try: Improve one sentence to make it more descriptive.
